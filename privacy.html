Privacy Policy — Gita Quotes Uploader

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Who uses this app

Gita Quotes Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Bhagwad Gita Quotes YouTube channel, on their own computer. It is not offered to other users.

What data it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and Shorts to the owner's channel (YouTube Data API);
  • read the owner's channel name, video list, titles, thumbnails and view counts (YouTube Data API);
  • read view counts and estimated revenue for the owner's videos (YouTube Analytics API).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to schedule uploads and to show the owner how each video is performing. The sign-in token, upload schedule and settings are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.

YouTube and Google policies

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
