Gita Quotes Uploader

Gita Quotes Uploader is a personal tool used by the owner of the Bhagwad Gita Quotes YouTube channel. It runs only on the owner's own computer and does two things:

  • Uploads the channel's own videos and Shorts to YouTube on a daily schedule, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Shows the channel's own video views and estimated earnings, using the YouTube Analytics API.

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account.
